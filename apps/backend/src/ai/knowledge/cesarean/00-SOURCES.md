# Cesarean (C-section) Recovery Knowledge Base — Sources & Licence

This knowledge base is curated from **open / public-sector medical sources** for grounding the Hospital AI recovery assistant. All content is factual post-operative guidance, paraphrased and structured for retrieval, with attribution below.

## Why cesarean section
Across lower-middle-income countries (Uzbekistan included), **cesarean section is the single most common major surgical procedure — ~30% of all surgical volume** (Lancet Commission global-surgery systematic review, PMC10310578), driven by high birth rates in Central Asia. It is also an ideal fit for a multi-week recovery-monitoring platform (wound care, pain, activity limits, bleeding, infection risk, postpartum mood).

# C-Section Pain Relief & Activity

## Pain relief
- Expect to need pain medication for the **first few days up to ~2 weeks**; tenderness can linger **3+ weeks**.
- **Paracetamol and ibuprofen** are commonly recommended and are generally suitable while breastfeeding.
- **Avoid aspirin and codeine-based painkillers while breastfeeding** unless a clinician advises otherwise.
- Always check medication safety with your provider if breastfeeding.

## Activity restrictions
- **Do not lift anything heavier than your baby** for the first **6–8 weeks**.
- **Do not drive for at least 2 weeks**, and **never drive while taking narcotic/opioid pain medication**. (NHS guidance: wait until you can perform an emergency stop comfortably and your insurer agrees.)
- **Avoid strenuous activity** — jogging, sit-ups, heavy housework — until cleared, typically resuming most activity in **4–8 weeks**.
- **Wait to resume sex** until you feel comfortable, usually around **6 weeks**.

## What helps
- **Gentle daily walking** aids healing and **reduces the risk of blood clots** — but build up slowly.
- Support your abdomen (e.g., a pillow) when coughing, laughing, or moving.
- Rest when the baby rests; accept help with chores and older children.
- Stay hydrated and eat well to support healing and (if breastfeeding) milk supply.

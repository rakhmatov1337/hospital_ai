# C-Section Wound & Scar Care

## Daily care
- **Gently clean and dry the wound every day.** Wash it with **mild soap and water**; pat dry.
- **Change the dressing daily**, or sooner if it becomes wet or soiled, until your provider says you no longer need one.
- Wear **loose clothing and cotton underwear** to avoid rubbing the wound.

## Bathing
- **Showering is fine** even with stitches, staples, or skin glue.
- **Avoid baths, hot tubs, and swimming for about 3 weeks** (or until the wound has healed and your provider approves).
- **Steri-Strips** (thin tape) usually **fall off on their own within ~10 days** — do not pull them off.

## The scar
- The scar is usually a **horizontal line about 10–20 cm long, just below the bikini line** (occasionally a vertical scar below the navel).
- Initial **redness fades over time**. On darker skin it may settle as a brown or white mark.

## Stitches/staples
- **Non-dissolvable stitches or staples are removed around day 5–7.** Dissolvable stitches do not need removal.

> If the incision becomes increasingly red, warm, swollen, painful, leaks pus/fluid, or opens up — this may be a wound infection. See warning signs.
